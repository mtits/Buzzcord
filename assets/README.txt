Buzzcord - a lightweight, old-school messenger for Discord
by Marc Turtal - https://app.marcturtal.com/buzzcord/

GETTING STARTED
1. Keep all these files together in one folder (for example Documents\Buzzcord).
2. Run Buzzcord.exe. It's signed by Marc Turtal; if Windows says "Windows protected
   your PC", click More info, check the publisher is Marc Turtal, then Run anyway.
3. Sign in with the QR code: Discord on your phone > your profile picture > Scan QR Code.

PLEASE READ
Buzzcord is unofficial. It isn't made, endorsed or supported by Discord Inc.
Discord's Terms of Service don't allow third-party clients on user accounts, so
Discord could limit or ban accounts that use it. Use it at your own risk.

Never paste a token, script or "login code" someone sends you: whoever has your
token controls your account. Buzzcord never asks for your password.

Your sign-in stays on this PC, encrypted for your Windows user, in
%APPDATA%\Buzzcord. No ads, no tracking, no telemetry.

Only download Buzzcord from https://app.marcturtal.com/buzzcord/ or
https://github.com/mtits/Buzzcord/releases
